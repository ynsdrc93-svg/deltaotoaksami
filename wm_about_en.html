WM - EUROPE'S NUMBER 1 IN VEHICLE PARTS TRADING

As a leading automotive parts wholesaler, we play a decisive role in shaping the European independent automotive aftermarket.

With the WM Fahrzeugteile sales brand, we are Europe's leading retailer of automotive parts and accessories, tools, workshop equipment and workshop concepts. The figures speak for themselves: in 2023, the group of companies generated sales of more than 2 billion euros, stocks over 250,000 catalogued items at around 230 locations in 8 countries and can look back on over 100 years of experience and passion for all things automotive.

WM – THE IDEAL PARTNER FOR THE MODERN VEHICLE WORKSHOP

  • Good service makes the difference: with our attractive tools and services, workshops are optimally equipped to meet the demands of the market and the wishes of their customers.

  • From A for wheel alignment to Z for ignition - we offer a high-quality full range of more than 250,000 automotive spare parts for all common vehicle models.

  • With more than 200 WM stores in Europe and the USA, we represent and pick around 45 million items a year fully automatically.

  • High availability and fast delivery of the required repair parts are crucial for the success of the vehicle workshop. Our modern logistics concept fulfils both.

  • The right concept for every garage: Our well-known garage brands stand for quality and create trust among drivers.

  • We make just-in-time deliveries to our customers several times a day.
